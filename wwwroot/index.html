How to play

Match two identical tiles (any flower with any flower, any season with any season) to remove them — a hand lifts both away.

Free tiles: nothing on top, and the left or right side open. Grey tiles are blocked.

Twists: ★ gold tiles give bonus points · ❄ blue tiles are frozen until you match tiles beside them · a wrong match costs moves · x4 combos and cleared layers earn 💣 bombs.

Controls: tap a tile to select · 🎥 changes the fixed camera view (no free rotation) · pinch or ＋－ = zoom · drag = move the board · 💡 hint · 🔀 shuffle · ↩ undo. 300 levels in 6 packs, plus Quick play and vs Computer.
